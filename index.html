Personal branding, explained

A personal brand isn't a logo.

It's what people remember about you, and whether they can say it in one sentence. It comes down to three things.

01

Position

What are you known for, in one line?

If someone can't repeat what you do after one visit to your page, they can't refer you. Pick the lane, say it plainly, and put it in your bio, your first line and your intro.

02

Proof

Show the work. Don't just describe it.

Real results, real clients (with their permission), real behind-the-scenes. One honest example beats ten adjectives. No proof yet? Show your process.

03

Presence

Show up on camera, on a rhythm.

People trust faces they've seen more than once. A recurring series, posted on a schedule you can actually keep, beats a burst of posts and then silence.

Most people are strong in one and missing the other two. DM us your handle and we'll tell you which one we'd fix first.
